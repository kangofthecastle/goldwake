# Sparse-detail landmark prompts

Built with the built-in image-generation tool in `style-transfer` mode. For
each asset, the original landmark was the geometry authority and the matching
`sparse-detail` environment was the style authority.

## Shared contract

- Preserve the original wide canvas, two separate side structures, approximate
  outer silhouettes, and completely open central flight channel.
- Match the active environments: broad clean matte planes, 4–6 readable values,
  slightly irregular hand-drawn silhouettes, warm near-black contours, and
  sparse controlled accent lines.
- Keep only details that explain an object: a structural band, fracture seam,
  eye or collar mark, banner fold, inlay, or grouped crystal facet.
- Render on perfectly flat `#ff0000` for local background removal.
- No photorealism, 3D rendering, global grain, mottling, material texture,
  brick-by-brick masonry, scratches, filigree, tiny repeated props, text, UI,
  enemies, bullets, shadows, or watermark.

## S1 — Bronze crossing

Restyle the paired flooded bronze ruins to match
`s1-02-flooded-colonnade.png`. Preserve the broken colonnades and colossal
bronze arm on each side. Use deep teal-black stone, oxidized green bronze,
muted bronze-gold, and small orange accents. Retain only two broad column bands,
two or three fracture seams, one simple arm-joint ring, and a few large chips
per side.

## S2 — Funerary crossing

Restyle the paired funerary platforms to match
`s2-02-processional-kings.png`. Preserve one seated Anubis guardian, one large
obelisk, and two cyan braziers per side. Use royal lapis, papyrus ochre, warm
sandstone-gold, deep blue-black, and restrained cyan. Retain only one eye or
collar mark per guardian, two obelisk inlay bands, one winged-disc motif per
platform, and simple flat flames.

## S3 — Jade crossing

Restyle the paired inward-curving bridge approaches to match
`s3-02-jade-causeway.png`. Preserve one pavilion tower, broad railings, a
lion-head terminal, one violet banner, and one grouped crystal mass per side.
Use dark jade, muted turquoise, orchid violet, pale ivory, and controlled gold.
Retain only two deck bands, two banner folds, one lion face mark, one tower-roof
rim, and grouped crystal facets per side.
